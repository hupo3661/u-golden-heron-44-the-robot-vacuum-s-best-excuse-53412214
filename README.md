The funniest one-line excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot, 140 characters or fewer, written deadpan by the machine itself:

> My map has marked that corner 'here be dragons.' I do not clean dragons. I have a bumper, not a sword.

## What it is

A robot vacuum explains why one corner stayed dusty: its own map flagged the corner as dragon territory, and a bumper is not a sword. The joke is rooted in the vacuum's own world — its map, its bumper — and lands on the first read with the turn at the end.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This prints the excuse and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
